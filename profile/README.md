### ⚡ About us

**L2** stands for the two Lorenzos behind it: two electronic engineers designing hardware from the chip level to the board level.  
We build open-source projects focused on custom PCB design, analog electronics and embedded systems.

---

### 🔧 What we do

- Custom PCB Design (schematic, layout, manufacturing files)
- Analog & Mixed-Signal Electronics
- Embedded Systems & FPGA
- Open-Source Hardware Projects
